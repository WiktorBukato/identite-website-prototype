Solutions / Digital trust and AI

When imitation gets easier, trust needs a stronger basis.

AI changes who can appear convincing and what software can do on our behalf. Identité addresses two distinct moments: verifying the requesting service and asking a person to approve a sensitive action.

Explore the two trust questionsTry an agent-action approval
Software prepares. The person decides.

The imitation may be someone your user trusts.

Consider a message that appears to come from a bank, a physician, an executive or a public agency. The person behind the account may be real; the party asking them to act may be the imitation.

The question changes from “Is this really our user?” to “How does our user know it is really us?” FDA answers at the authentication interaction: the enrolled app verifies the authentication server and the person checks the request. It provides a basis for service verification when a convincing appearance is not enough.

A real agent may still need your approval.

Now consider an authorized AI agent preparing a payment or another sensitive business action. The agent can prepare the work while the responsible person keeps the final decision.

The system owner defines the approval checkpoint. NoPass takes the request to the person’s phone, and the application waits for the device confirmation before proceeding.
