Products / NoPass

Trust at sign-in. Confidence at the next decision.

Give employees passwordless access and give people a clear way to approve sensitive operations. NoPass connects mutual authentication, device confirmation and central administration.

People. Devices. Access.

A valid account is only part of the interaction.

An employee needs to reach their work. A bank customer needs to recognize an approval request. Both deserve more than a familiar-looking screen asking them to trust it.

With Full Duplex Authentication, the enrolled app checks the authentication server and presents the visual comparison for the person’s decision. The organization verifies the user while the person has a way to check the requesting service.

Use NoPass where a person’s decision matters.

The approval capability also applies beyond the workforce: a bank can ask its customer to confirm a transaction, or an application can ask an owner to approve an AI-agent action.

Keep people and devices manageable.

The administration console brings users, devices and authentication activity together. Teams can enroll users, manage registered devices, set authentication factors and remove access when a device is lost or an account is no longer needed.

That gives the support team a place to resolve access problems and gives the person using NoPass a defined route back to their work.

Add confirmation before the operation proceeds.

Your system chooses the sensitive operation and responsible person. NoPass brings the request to that person’s phone; the application continues according to the approval result.

Connect to the environment you operate.

NoPass supports configured federation and RADIUS access paths, Windows Desktop Unlock, and application integration. The installed service can run on your infrastructure, including on-premises and private-cloud environments; cloud-service options depend on the product edition.

Keep your application’s business permissions and choose the authentication or approval point where NoPass participates.
