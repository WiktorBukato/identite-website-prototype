Keep trust close to the decisions that matter.

Help employees reach their work and keep sensitive decisions with the people responsible. NoPass brings mutual authentication and explicit operation approval to enterprise workflows.

Keep the decision with its owner.

An employee can be real while a request is misleading.

A familiar application asks for access. A message appears to come from an executive. A software agent prepares a consequential change. Verifying the employee is essential, and the employee also needs a basis for trusting what they are asked to approve.

Give the employee both verification and a decision.

FDA lets the enrolled app verify the authentication server and presents the comparison for the employee to check. This connects the authentication experience to the service and request, with an explicit human choice.

Hold a privileged action for its owner.

For example, an internal application can require approval before granting elevated access. It initiates NoPass confirmation and waits for the responsible person’s device response. An AI agent reaching a configured sensitive operation can use the same approval capability.

The application retains its permissions and execution rules. The owner decides which actions require this extra confirmation.
