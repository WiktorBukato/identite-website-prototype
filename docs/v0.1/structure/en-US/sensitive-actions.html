NoPass / Operation approval

The operation can wait for the person.

A successful sign-in does not settle every later decision. Use NoPass to ask the responsible person to confirm a banking transaction, business operation or AI-agent action before it proceeds.

Explore the approval flowOpen the banking demo ↗
Your bank. Your decision.

Keep a meaningful decision with its owner.

The system owner configures which operation requires approval and who should receive the request. This can be a bank customer confirming a transaction or an employee authorizing an action prepared by an application.

FDA brings the enrolled-device verification and visible comparison to that moment. The person can recognize the request and decide whether it should continue.

Make the response part of the operation.

The application waits for approval. Its integration also handles a decline, no response or an unavailable device. Those outcomes let the application stop or defer the pending action according to the owner’s policy.

For this operation flow, acceptance happens on the phone. The BigCommerce LoginFree sign-in example uses acceptance in the browser.
