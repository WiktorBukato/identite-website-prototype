PasswordFree / WordPress

A simpler return to your WordPress site.

Let customers reach their account through the PasswordFree plugin, with a passwordless experience backed by Full Duplex Authentication.

Get the WordPress plugin ↗Read the setup guide ↗
Back to the reason they came.

Help visitors reach the account they came for.

A returning visitor may want an order, a service or account information. PasswordFree adds a sign-in route that uses the authentication relationship established during registration.

The app-based FDA approach lets the enrolled app verify the authentication service and brings a visible check into the person’s decision.

Full Duplex Authentication →

Install and connect the plugin.

Open the official WordPress listing and follow the installation guide to connect your site to PasswordFree. The guide covers the plugin activation and service setup.

Open the WordPress plugin listing ↗Read the WordPress installation guide ↗

Choose the right hosting route.

On a self-hosted WordPress site, install through your site administration. On WordPress.com, use a plan that supports installing the plugin. Your administrator needs access to activate it and configure the service.

Registration, return and recovery belong together.

Enable the authentication options for your site and give customers the corresponding account-recovery route. The plugin exchanges email and site information with PasswordFree; the listing and your privacy information explain that service connection.
