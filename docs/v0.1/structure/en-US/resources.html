Questions that make an evaluation useful.

QuestionWhat to examine
How do both sides establish trust?The service/app relationship and the supported authentication flow.
What does the person see and decide?The request, comparison and accept/decline experience.
What happens on the first visit or a device change?Enrollment, account restoration and the support route.
How does the application use the result?Access permissions or operation handling, including a declined or unanswered request.
What changes for the people using and supporting it?Completion, unclear steps and recovery effort in a representative workflow.
