PasswordFree® / Shopify

Give Shopify customers a passwordless way back.

PasswordFree provides a passwordless customer-account experience for the legacy customer-account setup documented in its Shopify guide. For a store using Shopify’s new customer accounts, check the integration fit before installing.

Back to the reason they came.

Start with your customer-account model.

The published PasswordFree installation guide uses Shopify legacy customer accounts. Shopify’s new customer accounts use a different architecture; do not change your store’s account model just to follow an older guide.

Make the return to an account straightforward.

The app’s customer sign-in experience uses QR and picture-based authentication with configured device confirmation. Returning customers use their established authentication relationship to get back to the store.

Match your customer-account setup.

The published PasswordFree setup uses Legacy customer accounts and enables the sign-in buttons through the theme’s app embeds. Shopify has deprecated Legacy accounts in favor of its newer customer accounts.

If you use the newer accounts or are planning a migration, contact Identité® for the applicable integration before changing your store’s sign-in. The Legacy setup guide describes that specific configuration.
