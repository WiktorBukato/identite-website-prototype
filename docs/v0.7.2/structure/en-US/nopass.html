Trust at sign-in. Control at the next decision.

Give employees a clear way to authenticate and your team the tools to manage access. NoPass also supports integrated approval requests where an application needs a person’s decision before continuing.

Use NoPass™ where a person’s decision matters.

The approval capability also applies beyond the workforce: a bank can ask its customer to confirm a transaction, or an application can ask an owner to approve an AI-agent action.

Full Duplex Authentication® connects this access journey to the enrolled app’s authentication-service relationship. The employee can check the intended request, while the application keeps control of permissions.

Keep people and devices manageable.

The administration console brings users, devices and authentication activity together. Teams can enroll users, manage registered devices, set authentication factors and remove access when a device is lost or an account is no longer needed.

That gives the support team a place to resolve access problems and gives the person using NoPass a defined route back to their work.

  • See people and their enrolled devices together.
  • Review the relevant account and device state.
  • Keep administrative actions with the responsible IT team.
