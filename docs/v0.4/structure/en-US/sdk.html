Integrations / PasswordFree SDK

Bring mutual authentication into your application.

Give your customers a passwordless account experience within the application you build. The PasswordFree SDK connects registration, authentication and account restoration to the Full Duplex Authentication service.

Your application. A trusted experience.

Keep authentication and application permissions separate.

Your application owns its account, session and authorized actions. The supported SDK participates in registration, authentication and restoration; use the integration contract for the selected client and backend.

The application owns the final access decision.
