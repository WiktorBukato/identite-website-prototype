LoginFree: start with the store’s QR code.

In the BigCommerce app experience, an enrolled customer selects PasswordFree and scans the store’s QR code with their phone. They compare the black picture and number on the two screens, then accept in the browser. Their account opens and the phone shows a green confirmation.

LoginFree removes the email-entry step from this returning-customer flow. The customer still checks the request and chooses whether to continue.

Different ways to return.

The merchant enables the experience supported by their integration. App enrollment, browser recognition and device changes have different requirements.

Different ways to return.
ExperienceStart and decisionDevice context
Classic app flowAn identifier starts a request; the enrolled phone supplies the configured confirmation.An enrolled app and a supported service integration.
LoginFree app flowScan the QR code, compare the picture and number, then accept or decline in the browser.The BigCommerce guide illustrates this path. On the same phone, use the configured app link instead of scanning your own screen.
Appless returnA supported remembered-browser path lets an enrolled customer return without the phone interaction.Availability and recovery depend on the integration.
