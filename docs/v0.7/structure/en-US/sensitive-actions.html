NoPass™ / Operation approval

Approve the operation, not just the sign-in.

A successful sign-in does not settle every later decision. Use NoPass to ask the responsible person to confirm a banking transaction, business operation or AI-agent action before it proceeds.

Keep a meaningful decision with its owner.

The system owner configures which operation requires approval and who should receive the request. This can be a bank customer confirming a transaction or an employee authorizing an action prepared by an application.

Your application owns the operation.

The application owns what happens next.

NoPass supplies the authentication or approval result. The connected application owns permissions, the held operation and its execution policy.

  1. Prepared

    The application defines the action and responsible person.

  3. Waiting

    The application holds the action while requesting a decision.

  5. Decision

    The person accepts or declines using the configured experience.

  7. Application policy

    Approval is an input to the decision to proceed, not a replacement for business permissions.
