What is PasswordFree® for Shopify and how to install and set it up for quick passwordless logins?

Veronica Chizh · Published 2025-01-31

Since its inception in 2019, Identité has been at the forefront of authentication innovation. With a dedicated development team spanning the U.S. and Europe, we've introduced solutions tailored for diverse platforms. 


Our enterprise-grade product, NoPass, has set benchmarks in passwordless security. (See how it works) Building on this foundation, we've developed PasswordFree integrations for passwordless login on BigCommerce and WordPress, continually refining our offerings. 


The launch of PasswordFree for Shopify represents the culmination of our expertise, providing store owners with a mature and sophisticated passwordless authentication plugin.


From this article, you will know why you should give a try with PasswordFree if you're a store owner on Shopify and how to install and set up our app.

Olga Zhilko, Lead Business Analyst, walks you through the installation and setup process for passwordless logins


Q: Olga, could you guide us through the installation of PasswordFree on Shopify?

Olga Zhilko: Absolutely! Here's a concise guide:


  • Install PasswordFree:

    Navigate to the PasswordFree app listing on the Shopify App Store.

  • Click "Install" and confirm the installation.

  • Await the installation confirmation message.



Configure Customer Settings:

  • In your Shopify admin panel, go to "Settings."

  • Select "Customer Accounts."

  • Ensure the "Show Login Links" toggle is enabled.

  • Choose the "Legacy" tab under Customer Accounts.


Enable PasswordFree:

  • Return to the PasswordFree page in your Shopify admin.

  • Click "Enable PasswordFree."

  • You'll be redirected to the App Embed section.

  • Activate the PasswordFree toggle.



  • Adjust additional settings as needed.

  • Return to the PasswordFree page to finalize the setup.


Once these steps are completed, PasswordFree will be active, allowing customers to register and log in without the need for passwords, thereby enhancing both security and user experience.
