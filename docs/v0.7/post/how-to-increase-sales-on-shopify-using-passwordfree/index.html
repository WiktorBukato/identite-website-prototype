How to Increase Sales on Shopify: PasswordFree® for Secure and Seamless Shopping

Wiktor Bukato · Published 2025-02-06

How weak passwords and login friction impact your Shopify sales.

For Shopify merchants, sales performance is directly tied to customer experience and security. Yet, one of the biggest obstacles in online shopping remains largely overlooked: password-based authentication. While passwords have been the standard for decades, research shows they contribute to cart abandonment, lost revenue, and security vulnerabilities—issues that can directly impact a store’s success.


At Identité, we’ve spent years developing passwordless authentication solutions to eliminate these risks. Our latest innovation, PasswordFree for Shopify, is designed to help online store owners remove login barriers, improve security, and ultimately increase sales by reducing friction at checkout.

The Hidden Cost of Passwords: Lost Sales and Security Risks


Merchants often focus on marketing and product selection when looking for ways to increase Shopify sales, but many underestimate the impact of login friction and security concerns on conversion rates.


  • Consumers don’t trust password-based authentication methods the way they used to: While passwords remain the prevailing login and registration method, the consumers who tried biometrics have changed their preferences in favor of this innovative user authentication method.

  • Passwords are a top target for hackers: According to Verizon’s Data Breach Investigations Report, 81% of hacking-related breaches are caused by stolen or weak passwords. Cybercriminals exploit password reuse, phishing, and credential stuffing attacks to gain access to user accounts, leading to financial loss and reputational damage.

  • Cyberattacks force small businesses to close: A report from Business Dasher states that 60% of small and medium-sized businesses shut down within six months of a data breach. The combination of legal fees, lost customer trust, and regulatory fines can be too much to recover from.


How to Increase Sales by Removing Password Barriers


For many Shopify merchants, the login process is an overlooked conversion killer. Customers often abandon their shopping carts simply because they don’t want to go through a tedious login process or reset a forgotten password. Seamless login solutions have been proven to increase conversions by reducing friction.


Research from the Baymard Institute states that removing unnecessary steps from checkout—such as login prompts—can increase conversion rates by up to 35%. The simpler the authentication process, the more likely customers are to complete their purchases. 


This is where PasswordFree for Shopify makes a difference.
